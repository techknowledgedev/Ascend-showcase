ASCEND privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

ASCEND is a personal habit and self-improvement app.

What it stores. Your name, quests, progress, settings, books and rewards are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. ASCEND has no account system, no server, no advertising and no analytics. Nothing is uploaded.

Step counting. If you allow it, ASCEND counts your steps using the step counter built into your phone (Android's physical activity permission). It only counts steps. It does not see where you walk, never shares the count and never sends it off the device. You can remove the permission at any time in Android settings, and the app still works: you can type your steps in instead.

Sleep and heart rate. These are numbers you type in yourself. They are stored only on your phone, like everything else.

Backups. Backup files you create (or that the app saves to Downloads/ASCEND) are ordinary files on your phone. You decide where they go.

Notifications. If you turn on the daily reminder, the app posts a local notification. No data leaves your phone.

Deleting your data. Uninstalling the app removes its data. Backup files in Downloads/ASCEND are yours to delete.

Contact. Open an issue on GitHub.
