ASCEND privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

ASCEND is a private habit game for Android, made by Devesh Agarwal. This page says what the app stores, what it reads from your phone and what it asks Android for.

The short version. Everything stays on your phone. ASCEND has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics. It does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.

What it stores

Your name (if you enter one), quests, progress, settings, notes, books and rewards live in one file in the app's private storage on your phone, with a backup copy beside it. Nothing is uploaded. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Step counting

If you allow it, ASCEND counts steps with your phone's built-in step counter (Android's physical activity permission). It only counts steps. It does not see where you walk. You can take the permission away at any time in Android settings. The app still works, and you can type your steps in.

Health Connect

If you allow it, ASCEND reads data from Android Health Connect, only for the types you switch on: steps, distance, sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen and workouts. It uses them to show your day and to clear quests.

  • ASCEND only reads. It never writes to Health Connect.
  • The data is not sold, not shared with anyone and not used for advertising. It is not sent off your phone.
  • ASCEND reads recent days only, up to 14, and does not ask for access to older history.
  • About once an hour, ASCEND reads the latest numbers in the background, so your day is up to date when you open the app. This uses Health Connect's background-read permission.
  • You can take access away at any time in Health Connect settings. The app still works, and you can type the numbers in.

Numbers you type in

You can type in steps, sleep and resting heart rate yourself, for a day or night the phone or a watch did not cover. They are stored only on your phone, like everything else.

Reminders

Reminders and warnings are notifications made on your phone. Nothing is sent. Android may deliver a reminder a few minutes late to save battery.

Backups and reports

Backups and the weekly and monthly report PDFs are ordinary files that ASCEND saves in Documents/ASCEND on your phone. Older versions used Downloads/ASCEND. You decide where else to keep them and whether to share them. ASCEND does not upload them.

Permissions

ASCEND asks Android for the permissions below and no others. This list is checked against the app on every build. ASCEND does not ask for internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, calendar, phone, SMS or exact alarms.

Health Connect, read only

You choose which types to switch on. Android asks you before anything is read.

android.permission.health.READ_STEPS
Reads your steps, to show the day and clear step quests.
android.permission.health.READ_DISTANCE
Reads distance, to show it next to your steps.
android.permission.health.READ_SLEEP
Reads sleep sessions, to show last night and clear the sleep quest.
android.permission.health.READ_HEART_RATE
Reads heart rate, to show your average for the day on the Health tab.
android.permission.health.READ_RESTING_HEART_RATE
Reads resting heart rate, to show it on the Health tab and in the weekly report.
android.permission.health.READ_HEART_RATE_VARIABILITY
Reads heart rate variability, to show it on the Health tab and in the weekly report.
android.permission.health.READ_OXYGEN_SATURATION
Reads blood oxygen readings, to show the average in the weekly report.
android.permission.health.READ_EXERCISE
Reads workout sessions, to show the minutes and the number of workouts for the day.
android.permission.health.READ_HEALTH_DATA_IN_BACKGROUND
Lets the hourly update read the types above while the app is closed.

The phone

android.permission.ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION
Counts steps with the phone's own step counter. Asked only if you allow it.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Shows your reminders, warnings and the focus timer.
android.permission.VIBRATE
Short vibrations for taps and rewards.
android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
Sets your reminders and the hourly update again after the phone restarts.

Added by Android's work scheduler

ASCEND uses Android's work scheduler for the hourly update. The library adds these three on its own. ASCEND does not use the network, and it starts no service of its own.

android.permission.WAKE_LOCK
Keeps the phone awake for the few seconds the hourly update runs.
android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE
Lets the work scheduler run a task as a short service if it ever needs to. ASCEND declares no foreground service of its own.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
Lets the work scheduler ask whether a network exists before it runs a task that needs one. ASCEND's tasks need no network.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes its data. You can also reset your progress inside the app. It saves a copy first, so a mistake can be undone, and that copy is yours to delete too. Backups and reports in Documents/ASCEND are yours to delete. Health Connect data stays in Health Connect, where you manage and delete it.
