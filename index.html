ASCEND

How it plays

Your day, as a game.

01

Set your quests

Build a daily list: a workout, a step goal, focus time, a few pages, a line in your journal, or anything of your own.

02

Clear the day

Each quest pays XP and coins. Clear the list and the day counts toward your streak. A freeze can save a bad day.

03

Level up

XP becomes levels, levels become ranks from E up to S, and milestones unlock titles with their own crests.

What's inside

More than a checklist.

Quests

Daily quests

Set the list once. Quests pay XP and coins, grow a linked stat and keep your streak alive.

Dungeon

Gates and bosses

Seven-day gates with a boss and a strike quest each day. Harder gates open as you level.

Rewards

Coins and a reward shop

Copper, silver and gold. Stock the shop with your own rewards and spend what you've earned.

Health

Health, kept simple

Steps are counted on your phone and clear quests for you. Sleep and heart rate you type in yourself. No watch, no extra app, nothing leaves the phone.

Insight

Insights and monthly review

Patterns show up after two weeks of history. Each month gets a one-page review with a PDF.

Train

Focus, books and journal

A focus timer, a book log and a one-line nightly journal, each feeding your totals.

Reminders

Widgets and notifications

Home-screen widgets and reminders you control, all scheduled on the phone itself.

Feel

Sound and motion

Splash, badge and coin sounds are generated from maths, with a volume control and an off switch.

Safe

Backups you keep

Backups are ordinary files on your phone and you decide where they go. A gentle nudge reminds you to make one.

Access

Built for TalkBack

Screens are labelled so a screen reader can read every card, button and number.

Private by design

Your data never leaves your phone.

ASCEND keeps your name, quests, progress and rewards in the app's private storage. Uninstalling removes them.

Under the hood

Small, native and tested.

Native AndroidWritten in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose. Runs on Android 9 and newer.
Logic you can trustThe game rules are plain Kotlin, checked by hundreds of automated tests.
Automated buildsEvery build is compiled by GitHub Actions, so there is no hand-made installer.
Made, not downloadedSound effects are synthesised in code and the fonts, Orbitron and Rajdhani, are open source.
